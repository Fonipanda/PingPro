<product_requirements>
The goal is to build PingPro, a full-stack web application for AI-driven analysis of table tennis matches. Users upload a video of a match, specify which player to analyze and their skill level, and receive a comprehensive performance breakdown.

The core functionality revolves around two AI models:
1.  **GPT-4o Vision:** Provides high-level tactical analysis, personalized advice, and textual summaries based on video frames.
2.  **TTNet Model:** Performs low-level, frame-by-frame analysis to detect the ball, player movements, and key events like bounces, net hits, and serves.

The analysis results must be presented in a structured, multi-tab interface:
*   **Match Compilation:** A video of the full match with dead time removed, accompanied by statistical graphs and a summary.
*   **Strengths:** A highlight reel of the player's best moments, with specific stats and commentary on what they did well.
*   **Weaknesses:** A compilation of points to improve upon, with stats on faults and targeted advice.
*   **Best Rallies:** A video compilation of the most exciting exchanges.

All textual analysis must use a standardized, technical table tennis lexicon provided by the user. The application should feature a modern, professional, and responsive user interface.
</product_requirements>
